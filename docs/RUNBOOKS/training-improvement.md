# Training diagnostics and proposals

`diagnose <registry.sqlite> <training-experiment-id>` writes an immutable deterministic report. `diagnosis-report <registry.sqlite> <diagnosis-id>` reads and verifies it. Both require an existing registry. Schema7 stores diagnoses; schema8 stores training reviews. Both migrate older registries transactionally; online backup and registry integrity include diagnosis evidence.

Only explicitly tagged standalone training or the durable loop's exact training-window protocol is accepted, under current engine/indicator/signal versions. The generic tick-verification stage alone is insufficient: scientific OOS uses that stage too. Scientific protocols, including their prior-training receipts, and independent/final evaluation receipts cannot feed adaptation. Existing registered protected-range safeguards remain mandatory for any later child execution.

Reports bind the parent experiment key, immutable parent result hash, diagnostic build identity, seed and bounded options. Recorded no-signals, losses, rejected entries, absent closed trades and unresolved outcomes are factual diagnostics. Failure and unresolved evidence produce no child proposals. Legacy failed receipts may contain only an error; no missing strategy or outcome is inferred. Profitable completed training can receive bounded neighborhood review without any improvement claim.

Strategy-only stop/ATR/rule probes keep the parent's side, timeframe and entry policy. Costs, profiles, risk fractions and payoff rules are not relaxed. A seed-hash ordering and canonical concept deduplication select up to eight proposals by default, with an API ceiling of sixteen. Cost stress is excluded. Proposals carry operator, concept and immutable edge identities; the report binds their parent, seed and build. Their status is UNEVALUATED. They neither create experiments nor increment simulation counts. Exact retries/restart reuse a report; concurrent writers deduplicate publication.

`review-training <input-config.json> <registry.sqlite> <diagnosis-id>` executes a bounded review; `training-review-report <registry.sqlite> <review-id>` verifies/reads it. The input config uses the research command's format and exactly one dataset. Supply the parent's exact feature-cell setting in `limits.feature_cells` (the standalone `experiment` command uses 4000000). JSON/native representations can share identity. Dataset content, provenance, profile, costs, conversion sequence and feature configuration must exactly match the parent. The original training interval/protocol is reused; the parent baseline and all proposals execute under the current build. Every experiment uses normal protected-range registration and fenced/cache-aware evaluation.

Reviews retain baseline and child experiment/result hashes, proposal edges and exact signed net differences only when both outcomes are resolved. Proven no-signals contributes zero net; failed or unresolved results have no comparable net difference. They remain DESCRIPTIVE_TRAINING_ONLY. Cached results recover work committed before review publication without extra attempts. Reviews and all linked receipts participate in integrity checks and online backup. Execution is capped at sixteen children, four million feature cells and two million events, plus configured CLI event/state budgets. No improved/validated status is issued.

This is bounded explicit review, not the completed adaptive research loop. Opt-in bounded one-worker scheduling now persists jobs; see research-loop.md. The worker advances at most one missing experiment per checkpoint, preserving pause/lease checks between children. Richer failure diagnosis, acceptance rules and diversity remain pending. No diagnostic report can qualify a strategy. Real data approvals, independent inference, coverage and final acceptance remain separate prerequisites.

Diagnostic method v2 optionally adds a bounded training receipt breakdown: exact closed count/wins/losses/breakevens/net, closed commissions, strict consecutive negative-exit streak (zero or positive resets it), exit-year and UTC six-hour entry bins, exit reasons, entry statuses and rejection reasons. Account cash/marks/drawdown are explicitly recorded replay fields, not independent conversion/equity recomputation. Empty trades have no win-rate/expectancy estimate; unresolved outcomes and independent sample/coverage/profile limitations remain explicit. Summaries cap one million receipts per list,128 exit years,64 distinct reason labels and1024 bytes per reason, failing explicitly rather than silently truncating. No inference/acceptance or broker-session claim follows.

Older v1 reports omit this optional field, retain their recorded method/build identities and remain hash-readable. VERIFICATION/training-diagnosis-v1-fixture.json was produced by the actual pre-extension CLI binary and is used by the compatibility test. A new diagnosis/build creates a distinct report rather than rewriting existing evidence.

Diagnostic v3 prioritizes one `declared-friction-minimum-stop` hypothesis when an eligible resolved parent actually records the executable-side protective-stop minimum rejection. The proposed stop floor is profile minimum ticks plus declared spread-floor ticks and applicable entry-slippage ticks (zero entry slippage for passive limits). The floor must strictly increase and fit the one-million-tick grammar budget. It preserves the parent entry/ATR policies, profile, costs and risk rules, consumes one child slot and participates in canonical deduplication. Other probes retain seeded ordering. Wider actual spreads, gaps, minimum volumes and margin can still reject it; only replay establishes a child outcome. Pending contracts are never repriced or resized. The exact synthetic30->41 fixture and same-context review are covered by tests; they make no independent improvement/qualification claim. Historical v1/v2 receipts remain immutable.
