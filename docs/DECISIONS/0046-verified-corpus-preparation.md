# Verified corpus preparation

Corpus replay now builds inspection fingerprints and bounded closed candle contexts in one verified source traversal. `Prepared` owns an immutable report and frames for the chosen primary timeframe and every higher timeframe. Fields are private and the snapshot cannot be deserialized. It exposes borrowed candles and causal strategy masks. The aggregate candle cap is checked before every retained candle; quote residency remains one decoded artifact block. Inspection alone retains no candle vectors and preserves its report shape and identity.

Replay borrows the primary frame for signals, initial stops and account transitions. This replaces three separate internal preparation traversals with one and removes a duplicate primary-frame vector. Registry key preflights and account replay still perform their own validations. Every consumed artifact is still fingerprinted; no process-wide cache or current-byte eligibility is inferred from an old snapshot. Saved account prefixes are still recomputed and compared, including full receipts and state. This is not fast-seek recovery, and real/protected/non-USD execution remains refused.

Three integration tests compare all eight instruments and seven primary timeframes against independent materialized candles, exact aggregate budgets, native/blocked layouts, higher-frame causal masks and mutation refusal. Existing complete market/stop/limit receipt, registered CLI resume and blocked corruption tests remain unchanged and pass. The full check and integrated browser/operations results are recorded in ACCEPTANCE.md.

`BENCHMARKS/prepared-pipeline-v1.json` records three rotated fresh-process pairs through the actual release corpus-batch and registry-check CLIs. All six runs retain identical full financial receipt hashes and 574 resolved trades; cached reuse and registry counts are checked separately. This small synthetic workload gives no general speed, RSS, qualification or production acceptance claim. Shared batch lifecycle, checkpoint-aware fast-seek, large-lifetime state and representative independent acceptance remain separate requirements.
