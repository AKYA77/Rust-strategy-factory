# Pending entries and initial margin — engine v2

One pending entry or one open position per strategy simulation. Pending quantity is fixed at signal publication. Stop entry levels are adverse offsets from the publication quote's effective ask (long) or bid (short); limit levels are favorable offsets. Both are on the instrument tick grid. Orders first become eligible at a subsequent source event, including sequence ordering within timestamp ties.

Placement estimates mark friction using the publication spread and applicable entry slippage, sizes on the volume grid, and records a reservation. Reservation is not a cash deduction: exclusivity prevents other entries from consuming it. At an eligible fill, fixed quantity is checked against current equity, currency conversion, actual executable mark and initial margin. A failed preflight cancels the intent and records RiskRejected, with no executed fill and no silent resizing. This is a versioned research acceptance model, not a claim about any broker's order acceptance rules.

Initial margin is rounded upward. Entry marked equity includes entry commission and spread/slippage loss. Intended stop loss includes adverse stop-exit slippage and both commissions, and must fit 10% equity. A bounded integer search finds the largest feasible volume step. This corrects v1 sizing, which allowed margin against cash before the immediate marked entry loss. Legacy market concept hashes remain stable; the changed execution version and executable identity invalidate old experiments. On the independent EURUSD fixture, volume changes from 19,980 to 19,210 micro-lots and 90-unit target P&L becomes $1.7289.

Existing positions and pending orders consume the event before newly published signals cause cancellations. A cancellation decided from a quote cannot undo that quote's triggered fill. Predetermined expiry precedes trigger eligibility. New orders are placed after event processing and cannot fill on their publication quote. Signals arriving while an order/position exists do not stack reservations. Expiry and cancellation release the reservation without cash flow.

Stops/targets are fixed from actual accepted entry at 1R/3R. Later stop gaps can exceed intended risk; losses are never capped. Maximum holding time starts at actual fill, not order placement. Pending lifetime is independently bounded to 24 hours by the grammar. Missing exit events and still-pending orders remain explicit. L1 full-fill assumptions remain; no depth, partial-fill or broker maintenance-margin/forced-liquidation rule is fabricated.
