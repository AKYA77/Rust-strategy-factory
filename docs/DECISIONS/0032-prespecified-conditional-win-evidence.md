# Prespecified conditional-win evidence

These are statistical evidence settings, not changes to10% intended financial risk. The method/version and policy enter protocol identity before OOS exposure; later retuning/removal refuses under the existing exposure reservation system. Absent fields preserve legacy plan/report serialization.

For resolved net-win indicator X, the evidence multiplier is1+lambda*(2X-1). Under the null that each outcome's conditional success probability given all previous information is at most1/2, this wealth process is a nonnegative supermartingale. Ville plus a union bound over a complete frozen family permits arbitrary dependence without assuming independent trades or candidates. This conditional null is stronger/different from a marginal/IID win-rate claim. Rejecting it is not a stationary win-rate interval, positive expectancy proof or future guarantee. Declared trials alone do not establish complete multiplicity accounting.

Only the final frozen horizon enters the gate. Early crossings remain diagnostics and cannot rescue a later failure. Counts determine final log evidence without repeated floating addition; a conservative numerical margin holds borderline comparisons. Log values remain finite when ordinary evidence overflows/underflows; unrepresentable values are absent. Breakevens count as nonwins. Unresolved/failed/annual/full-period/source/independent criteria remain separate. Every report explicitly denies qualification, including synthetic reports with strong statistical evidence.
